How it works

  1. Players. Pick the board (USA or Europe), then add each player's name and train color.
  2. Photo. At the end of the game, take one photo of the whole board from above, from any side of the table. Tap its 4 corners; drag any corner to fine-tune.
  3. Colors. Tap 2 trains of each player's color so the app learns your lighting.
  4. Routes. The app colors every claimed route. Tap a route to fix it. Enter the trains each player has left and it points out anything that doesn't add up.
  5. Tickets. Enter each player's destination tickets: completed ones add, missed ones subtract. Longest path is worked out from the board.
  6. Scores. Final totals, with the tie-breaker rule.

Photo tips

  • Hold the phone as flat over the board as you can, with the whole board and score track in frame.
  • Even light works best. Avoid lamp glare on the board and shadows across it.
  • Europe: routes are entered by length for now; the camera works on the USA board.
  • The game saves itself as you go. If the page reloads, use Resume.
  • Works offline: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once on Wi-Fi; after that it runs with no internet.

Tap any route to change who owns it. Enter how many trains each player actually has left; the app flags routes to check if the counts don't match.

Claimed routes

Destination tickets and bonuses

For each ticket, enter its points and tap Completed or Not completed. Tap a ticket to remove it.
