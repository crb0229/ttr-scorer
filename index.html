Ticket to Ride Scorer

Game setup

Board:

Photo of the board

Stand at the end of the table so the whole board is in frame, including the score track around the edge.

Tap the 4 outer corners of the board.

Show me each color

Tap 2 trains of each player's color on the board. Use trains that sit on a different painted color if you can.

Check the routes

Tap any route to change who owns it. Compare "trains left" with the trains each player actually has in front of them.

Claimed routes

Enter how many routes of each length each player claimed.

Destination tickets and bonuses

For each ticket, enter its points and tap Completed or Not completed. Tap a ticket to remove it.
